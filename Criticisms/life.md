what is the point of a life that is fully consumed by biological and social maintenance functions with no surplus left for self-construction.  


Curiosity is the non-negotiable substrate of a human life worth living. When curiosity is killed — by comfort, by conformity, by maintenance load — the person continues to exist biologically but terminates as an individual. 
Legacy is the externalization of self-construction: the proof that the individual existed as something more than a biological relay station passing genes and maintenance costs to the next generation.
