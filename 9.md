# Change
We fear it
but why should we change? this is the subjective question of our own experiences. 
Why should someone change for others around them?
What if that person is dropping their last bit of what makes them special. into a normal affirmative individual?
The concept of people being different isn't scary to digest. rather the concept of accepting ourselves before the people so we don't change as much is the scary part. 
People fall in love with you. And not in the ideal version of you in your head.
