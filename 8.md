There is something I call the age-bias dilemma. In your twenties, you are often told that you are still a child who understands nothing about the world. No matter what choices you make, they are frequently dismissed on the assumption that you lack the rational capacity to decide for yourself. What is rarely acknowledged is that no one ever has life completely “figured out.” Even at a later age, people continue to explore their identities, make mistakes, pick up new hobbies, and take risks. Often alluded to ones that would have been better taken earlier. Even seniors do not possess absolute clarity. In the process, we trade years of our lives trying to erase a presumed incompetence, driven by mistrust and the desire to cleanse ourselves of a stigma that should never have existed.

You're considered a persecuted breed if you try to break out of this toxic cycle
